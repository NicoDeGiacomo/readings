# About

I'm Nico, a Staff Backend Engineer with 11+ years of experience building systems at scale at companies like Mercado Libre and Kavak.

This site is my personal collection of summaries and takeaways from papers, books, and blog posts that shaped how I think about software engineering. It's not a comprehensive reading list -- it's a curated set of things I found genuinely valuable, with honest notes on why they matter and what I took away from them.

Every entry has three parts: **why you should read it**, **the key ideas**, and **my personal takeaways**. The last section is the one I care about most -- it's where I connect concepts to real experience, and it's what no link list can replicate.

I write in English to reach a broader audience, but I'm based in Buenos Aires, Argentina. If you want to connect, find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/NicoDeGiacomo) or [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/nicolasdegiacomo).
